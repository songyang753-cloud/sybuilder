## License boundaries and changes

- **Diagrammer:** `skills/product-flow/modules/diagramming/` derives from
  `yizhiyanhua-ai/fireworks-tech-graph` v1.0.5, commit
  `14be3ad3b05389a5d603562c207eb37157637127`, MIT. Local module discovery,
  rendering, compatibility checks and neutral style names were added/changed.
  Its original license remains in that directory; [audit details](docs/third-party-audit.md).
- **OWASP:** `skills/coding-standards/references/authority.md` section Z2,
  including the translated API1–API10 table and ASVS level explanation, is an
  adaptation of OWASP API Security Top 10 (2023) and ASVS by their project
  contributors. **That complete section, including local explanatory additions,
  is CC BY-SA 4.0**, not Apache-2.0. Preserve attribution, change notices and
  share-alike terms when adapting or redistributing it. Other independent
  sections are not relicensed by this statement.
- **Color guidance:** section 3 of `craft-layer.md` condenses/translates
  `skill.color-expert/SKILL.md`, compiled by @meodai, under CC BY 4.0.
  SYBuilder added its local rules and cross-references. The upstream's 140+
  third-party reference articles are **not bundled** and are not covered by
  that upstream skill's blanket license.
- **Engineering review guidance:** the small-change/reviewability guidance in
  `foundation.md` P1 translates and summarizes Google's Engineering Practices,
  CC BY 3.0. This is not an official Google translation.
- **Keyboard contracts:** `keyboard-contracts.md` and `keyboard-flows.json`
  include selected, translated and executable adaptations of the W3C ARIA
  Authoring Practices Guide by W3C and its contributors. Preserve the
  [W3C notice](licenses/w3c--aria-practices.txt). Local test cases and emphasis
  are modifications, not an assertion of W3C approval.
- **Design-theory reference:** `ZeroZ-lab/cc-design` declares MIT in its README
  but has no separate LICENSE at the inspected revision. The retained notice
  records that exact basis; no copyright year or missing upstream notice is invented.
- Other reviewed guidance was selected, condensed or translated; local examples,
  contracts and gates were added. It is not an unmodified redistribution of the
  complete source skills. MIT/Apache notices are retained even for closely related
  method references; inclusion of a notice does not imply bundling that project.

## Research references are not vendored libraries

The larger inventories in `skills/product-flow/references/external-sources.md`,
`skills/coding-standards/references/sources.md` and the review Skill record
research, rejected proposals, factual standards and optional tools. Listing a
repository, public threshold, engineering idea or book does not mean its source
code, article text, images or complete templates are included. Do not treat a
citation, star count or this manifest as permission to copy additional material.

Feishu/Lark CLI, DingTalk Workspace CLI, Figma MCP, browsers, D2, Mermaid and
optional review/model tools remain external implementations. Users install and
authorize them separately. Their accounts, logos, credentials, client binaries
and SDKs are not redistributed by SYBuilder. Python development packages listed
in `requirements-dev.txt` are installed from their distributors, not vendored.
